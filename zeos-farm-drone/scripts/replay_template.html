One farm drone, five behaviours, flown by Claude

A morning on a cattle farm, recorded from the live demo and played back here exactly as it ran. Every drone command was chosen by Claude; the farm's events were clicked on a schedule: a vehicle, straying cows, a leaking trough, a lost toolbox, a fire alert, a flat battery. The ZEOS kernel decided which behaviour flew the drone and when, and checked every command against the flight rules.
